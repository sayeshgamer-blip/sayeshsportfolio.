About

From a weekend favor to a full-time craft

The short version of how I got here — and what I'm trying to do for every creator and brand I work with.

I started editing videos five years ago as a hobby, helping a friend edit his YouTube uploads on weekends. There was no plan behind it — just a favor that turned into a Saturday habit.

Somewhere along the way I fell in love with storytelling through editing, sound design, and color grading. What started as helping out became the thing I wanted to get better at every single week, and eventually I turned it into a full-time career.

I don't just cut clips together — I build a narrative that keeps the viewer watching until the very end.

That's still the difference I try to make on every project. My mission is simple: help creators and brands turn raw, unorganized footage into polished stories that grow their audience.
